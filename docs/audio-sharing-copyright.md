# Audio sharing and copyright

Research checked 13 September 2026. Scope: Czech Republic/EU, personal listening. This is a technical product assessment, not a legal clearance for every recording, audience, or country.

The feature is not inherently infringing: it can carry recordings you own all relevant rights to or material licensed for the intended use. Its legality depends on the source, licence, and audience. Wi-Fi proximity or encryption does not turn redistribution into personal use.

Czech Copyright Act §§29–30 permits limited personal, noncommercial use and personal copies, subject to conditions. That does not give general permission to send recordings to friends, customers, or the public. §§43–44 separately protect technological measures and rights-management information. [Current Czech statute, §§29–30 and 43–44](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-121#p30).

EU Directive 2001/29/EC distinguishes reproduction from communication to the public. Its private-copying exception is conditional; it does not automatically authorize public transmission. Compressing a recording or extracting a video's soundtrack creates a further reproduction, so rights or an applicable exception are still needed. A temporary filename alone does not establish the temporary-copy exception. These are applications of Articles 2, 3, 5, and 6 to Medio's design. [Directive 2001/29/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32001L0029).

The Court of Justice held that the private-copying exception cannot cover copies from unlawful sources. Buying a subscription is also not, by itself, permission to redistribute its catalogue. Check the actual licence. [ACI Adam, C-435/12, judgment and particularly paragraph 58](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A62012CJ0435).

Medio's implementation reads ordinary local files and refuses assets marked as protected. It neither captures other apps' audio nor removes DRM. Compression affects only a temporary audio stream copy, retains available common metadata, and leaves the source untouched. These technical restrictions reduce misuse opportunities but cannot verify ownership, all licence terms, or whether a particular audience is legally private. Unprotected does not mean copyright-free.

For your own private listening, use lawfully sourced material within your rights or an applicable exception. For public listening, commercial use, distribution of copyrighted recordings, or a public product launch marketed around sharing third-party music, obtain a Czech copyright lawyer's review of the precise use and licences. Neither the sharing feature nor the compression option can be declared lawful in every situation from the code alone.
